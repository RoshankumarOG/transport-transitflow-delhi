Where does the data come from?

Loading network details…

Delhi Transport Department and DMRC GTFS data, republished by reclaim.city. Bus positions refresh every 30 seconds. Reports older than 3 minutes are hidden. No cars or trains are simulated.

How accurate are routes and times?

These are real published stops and route connections. Bus timetable durations are approximate; the metro feed’s service calendar ended in December 2025, so it describes network connections rather than current departures. Routes on the map connect stops and are not turn-by-turn directions. Travel times and historical fare estimates are not guaranteed.
